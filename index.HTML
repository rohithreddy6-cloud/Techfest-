ABOUT

Rohit Gurunath Sharma (born April 30, 1987) is an Indian cricketer and former captain of the India national cricket team, widely nicknamed "Hitman" for his explosive batting. He is a right-handed top-order batter who plays domestic cricket for Mumbai and represents Mumbai Indians in the Indian Premier League

Contribution

Rohit Sharma has made a historic contribution to Indian and world cricket as both a record-breaking opening batter and an ICC trophy-winning captain. Nicknamed the "Hitman," he redefined modern white-ball batting with his effortless six-hitting and ability to score massive double centuries, while guiding India to multiple global titles.

Comeback

The 2025 Fitness Transformation & ODI Return After retiring from T20Is in 2024 and Test cricket in mid-2025, Rohit dedicated a five-month break to overhauling his fitness. • Weight Loss and Conditioning. Training intensively in Mumbai, he shed 11 kilograms to extend his white-ball career. • Match-Winning Return in Australia. He silenced critics during the October 2025 ODI tour of Australia. After a gritty 73 in Adelaide, he struck an unbeaten 121 in Sydney to win the Player of the Series award.
• The 2011 World Cup Snub & 2013 Reinvention. Dropped from India's victorious 2011 ODI World Cup squad due to inconsistent middle-order form, Rohit bounced back in 2013 when captain MS Dhoni promoted him to open the batting. That move unlocked his potential, leading to three ODI double centuries. • Test Cricket Revival (2019). After years of struggling to cement a middle-order spot in red-ball cricket, he made a triumphant Test comeback as an opener against South Africa in 2019, scoring 176, 127, and a maiden double century (212) in the same series.
